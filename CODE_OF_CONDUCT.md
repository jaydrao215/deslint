# Code of Conduct

Deslint adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as the community standard for participation. The full text is
canonical and authoritative; this file records the project-specific
contact and enforcement details.

## Our Standards (summary)

Participants in the Deslint project — across issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and any related forum — agree to make the
community a welcoming, respectful, and professional space.

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback graciously.
- Take responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and
  learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community, not just yourself.

Conduct that is unwelcome — including harassment, personal attacks,
or deliberate disruption of discussions — has no place here.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the GitHub
repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, any official
chat or email channels, and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

To report a concern, email **conduct@deslint.com**. Reports will be
handled confidentially. Expect acknowledgement within 48 hours and a
substantive response within 7 days.

When reporting, include:

- Where the incident occurred (PR / issue / discussion link).
- What happened, with quotes or screenshots when possible.
- Any prior attempts to resolve it directly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the
standards above. Responses to violations follow the
[Contributor Covenant Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines),
which escalate from a private correction to a temporary ban to a
permanent ban depending on severity and recurrence.
